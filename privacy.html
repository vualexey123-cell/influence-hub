2. What personal data we collect

We only collect the data you choose to give us and a limited amount of technical data needed to run the Website.

When you complete the "Book a demo" form, we collect: the venue name, the country, your name, and your contact details (phone number or messenger handle such as Telegram/WhatsApp). Submitting this form is voluntary and requires your explicit consent via the checkbox on the form.

c) Technical and usage data

If you accept analytics cookies, we collect standard technical data such as a truncated (anonymised) IP address, device and browser type, approximate location, referring pages, and the pages you view on the Website. We do not collect this analytics data unless you consent.

We do not intentionally collect any special categories of data (such as data on health, religion or political views), and we do not process payment card data on this Website.

3. Why we use your data and our legal basis

  • To comply with law and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims where necessary. Legal basis: our legitimate interests and legal obligations.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. We use:

  • Strictly necessary storage — a small entry in your browser's local storage that remembers your cookie choice and your language preference. This is required for the Website to function and does not track you.
  • Analytics cookies (optional) — set by Google Analytics only after you click "Accept" in the cookie banner. They help us count visits and see which pages are useful. If you click "Decline", no analytics cookies are set.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site, which will make the cookie banner appear again. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
