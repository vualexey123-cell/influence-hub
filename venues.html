A fresh line-up of local creators — every month, hands off

Register your restaurant or café and we do the rest: we source neighbourhood creators, agree the terms, spread their visits across the month, and send you one clear report. You just open the door.

  • → No-shows covered. If a creator doesn't deliver, we replace them the same month at no extra cost.
  • → A trial first month. Don't like the line-up? We stop after it — no renewal pressure.
