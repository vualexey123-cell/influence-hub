What is Influence Hub?

A simple bridge between you and the venues near you.

Restaurants and cafés want honest, local content — but they don't have time to find the right creators, reach out, and coordinate visits. That's the gap we fill. Venues sign up with us, and we invite creators like you to come in, enjoy a meal on the house, and share it with your audience.

For you it means a steady stream of invitations to genuinely good local spots, on terms that are clear before you go — no cold DMs, no haggling, no ad scripts. You keep full creative control and post in your own voice.

Creator FAQ

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining and being matched with venues is free for creators.

Do I get paid, or is it the meal?

Every collaboration is agreed upfront. Many are on a hosted-meal basis; some venues also offer a fee. Whatever the terms, you see them before you accept a visit.

How often will I get invitations?

It depends on your city and how well your audience fits the venues currently on the platform. We onboard by city and reach out when there's a good match.

Do I have to label it as an ad?

Yes — honest disclosure is required by advertising rules and protects you and the venue. Beyond that, the content is yours, in your own words.

What if I can't make a booked visit?

Just let us know in good time so we can reschedule. Reliability is what keeps invitations coming.

How big does my audience need to be?

There's no hard minimum — what matters most is a real, local, engaged audience. Micro-creators are very welcome.
